## Fixed

- Settings, the profile editor and the Welcome guide open in front of other apps' windows. Before, opening one from a shortcut, the `spacetile` command or the menu-bar panel could leave it behind the app you were using.
- Moving the pointer over a Spacetile window keeps focus there, instead of passing it to the window underneath.
- Focus follows mouse keeps working while a Spacetile window is focused. Before, it stopped until you clicked another app.
