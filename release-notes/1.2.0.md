## New

- Restarting Spacetile keeps your layouts. Each Desktop comes back with its windows in the tiles they had, floating windows included, the first time you show it. Settings changed while Spacetile wasn't running still apply: a Desktop whose layout config.json now sets differently takes that layout, and new float rules float their windows.

## Improved

- Focus follows mouse waits until the pointer rests on a window, so crossing the corner of another window on the way somewhere else leaves focus where it was.
- Settings, the profile editor and the Welcome guide open in front of other apps' windows. Before, opening one from a shortcut, the `spacetile` command or the menu-bar panel could leave it behind the app you were using.

## Fixed

- An open menu or popover no longer closes when the pointer moves over the part of it that covers another window.
- Focus follows mouse works across the whole screen with Bartender running. Other apps' overlays no longer stop it either; only menus, and the focused app's own popovers and panels, do.
- Pressing a focus key or ⌘⇥ just after the pointer crosses onto another window keeps the focus you chose. Before, the window under the pointer took focus a moment later.
- Moving the pointer over a Spacetile window keeps focus there, instead of passing it to the window underneath.
- Focus follows mouse keeps working while a Spacetile window is focused. Before, it stopped until you clicked another app.
- A window can now be resized or grown to fill its column or row when an empty tile from a place command sits beside it. Before, the empty tile always kept a strip of the space.
- Dropping a window into an empty tile a profile kept for an app no longer stops that app's next window from tiling.
