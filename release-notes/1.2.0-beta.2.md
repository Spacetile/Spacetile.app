## New

- Restarting Spacetile keeps your layouts. Each Desktop comes back with its windows in the tiles they had, floating windows included, the first time you show it. Settings changed while Spacetile wasn't running still apply: a Desktop whose layout config.json now sets differently takes that layout, and new float rules float their windows.

## Fixed

- Focus follows mouse works across the whole screen with Bartender running. Other apps' overlays no longer stop it either; only menus, and the focused app's own popovers and panels, do.
- Pressing a focus key or ⌘⇥ just after the pointer crosses onto another window keeps the focus you chose. Before, the window under the pointer took focus a moment later.
